Try a request against CRS

Pick a payload, an engine and a paranoia level. The request is sent from your browser to sandbox.coreruleset.org; what comes back is the list of rules that matched.

More options
Browsers refuse to send some headers from a page (Cookie, Host, Referer, Origin, Sec-*…); those are dropped silently, and Safari also drops User-Agent. Use curl for them.
